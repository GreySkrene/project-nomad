# Offline equipment library

This folder separates exact-model manuals from general safety guidance. A file
listed as an exact-model manual must match the model printed on the device.

## Confirmed exact models

- Jackery Explorer 600 v2, model JE-600A: complete 46-page official manual,
  manufacturer product guide, operating quick reference, solar-input limits,
  storage, and troubleshooting.
- Lenovo Legion Go, first generation 8APU1 / type 83E1: complete 40-page
  Lenovo-authored user guide, Lenovo PSREF product specifications, and a locally
  authored offline/power card. Lenovo's official support record points to the
  guide, but the saved copy came from a third-party mirror because Lenovo blocked
  automated retrieval; its model, metadata, and full content were verified.
- Midland ER10/ER10VP: stored separately under
  `../communications/radio/` with an offline quick-reference card.

## Confirmed equipment without model numbers

- Butane canisters, portable power banks, flashlight, and assorted batteries:
  only general safety and maintenance guidance belongs here until exact model
  numbers are recorded. Do not substitute a different product's manual.
