# Jackery Explorer 600 v2 (JE-600A) — emergency quick reference

Use this card with the complete manufacturer manual. Confirm the label says
**JE-600A**, 640 Wh, before following model-specific limits.

## Critical limits

- Battery: 640 Wh LiFePO4.
- AC output: 120 V, 60 Hz, **500 W total continuous**, 1,000 W surge.
- Solar input: one DC8020 port; **16–60 V working voltage, 10.5 A, 200 W max**.
- Vehicle input: **11–16 V, 8 A max**. Do not connect to a 24 V vehicle outlet.
- USB-C outputs: one 100 W max and one 30 W max. USB-C is output only; it does
  not charge this power station.
- The unit is not waterproof. Keep ports and ventilation openings dry and clear.

## Start and stop outputs

1. Press the main power button to wake the display.
2. Press **AC** only when an AC appliance is needed. The two outlets share the
   500 W limit.
3. Press **DC** for the 12 V vehicle-style output.
4. Disconnect loads and turn unused output groups off to conserve energy.

The energy-saving function can automatically shut down low-power loads after
several hours. Disable it when an essential low-draw device must run
continuously, then monitor remaining capacity manually.

## Solar charging

1. Read the solar panel label before connecting it. Its open-circuit voltage
   must remain within the Jackery's 16–60 V input range, including in cold sun.
2. Place the panel in unobstructed sun, aimed squarely toward the sun; avoid
   shade across even part of the panel.
3. Connect the panel to the DC8020 input and confirm the display shows input
   watts. Reposition if input is unexpectedly low.
4. Never connect panels in a configuration that can exceed 60 V or 10.5 A.
   Jackery supports one 100/200 W panel directly or two compatible 100 W panels
   through the specified parallel adapter. Do not guess at polarity or adapters.

## Storage and inspection

- Store dry and away from fire, heaters, direct sun, children, and corrosive
  materials.
- Jackery's manual recommends storing at roughly 30–50% charge, checking and
  recharging about every three months, and completing a charge/discharge cycle
  every 6–12 months.
- Inspect the case, vents, cables, and ports before use. Stop using it if it is
  swollen, cracked, wet, unusually hot, smells abnormal, or has damaged wiring.

## Troubleshooting

- Overload: disconnect all loads, verify their running and startup watts, then
  restart with a load below the 500 W total limit.
- Temperature warning: turn outputs off and let the unit return to its allowed
  operating temperature in a ventilated location.
- No solar input: verify sun, connectors, polarity, panel open-circuit voltage,
  and the 200 W input limit.
- Reset: Jackery's current support page says to hold the **main Power and USB
  buttons together for five seconds** until the screen turns off and relights.
- If an error remains, record the displayed code and consult the complete
  troubleshooting table; do not open the enclosure.
