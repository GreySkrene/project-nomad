Severe choking: child older than 1 year

Call 911 or direct someone else to call and retrieve an AED.

Give 5 back blows, followed by 5 abdominal thrusts. Repeat until the object comes out or the person becomes unresponsive.

If unresponsive, lower them safely and begin CPR. Remove an object only if you can see it.
