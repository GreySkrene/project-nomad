Emergency water disinfection

Exact instructions assembled from retrieved official passages.

First choice: use bottled water or boil when the advisory says boiling is appropriate. Chemical contamination cannot be fixed by boiling, bleach, or filtering.
1

Clarify the advisory

A boil-water advisory differs from a chemical-contamination warning.

2

If cloudy, settle and pre-filter

Use a clean cloth, paper towel, or coffee filter.

3

Boil for the stated time

CDC: 1 minute; 3 minutes above 6,500 feet. The older EPA sheet uses a different altitude threshold, so the conflict stays visible.

4

Cool and store covered

Let water cool naturally and use clean containers with covers.
